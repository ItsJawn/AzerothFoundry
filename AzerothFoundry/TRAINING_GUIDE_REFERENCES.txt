AZEROTH FOUNDRY - CURATED TRAINING GUIDE REFERENCES
v1.0.30 / WoW Forever Interface 16001 / 2026-11-09

Coverage: reference milestones 1-225 for Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Enchanting,
Engineering, Leatherworking, Tailoring, Cooking and First Aid.

Guides are original, compact Foundry milestone tables, reviewed against public
community skill-range and recipe-source references and intended for in-game
Forever verification. No other addon's code, graphics, layouts, databases, or
written descriptions are bundled or copied. Reference skill bands and craft
counts are editorial estimates, not a claim of an optimal cost or guaranteed
number of successful skill-ups.

Important limitations:
- A reference step is not proof a character knows the recipe or a specific
  Forever NPC teaches it. Live recipe records and ProfessionDB are required to
  verify the unlock and accurate reagents; unconfirmed steps are flagged.
- Vendor formulas, Expert books/manuals, special trainer rank requirements,
  and workstations exist for some professions. These cannot be honestly
  described as city-trainer-only routes. They have explicit acquisition notes.
- Enchanting may need an item/target for a native enchant action; a guide
  recommendation does not guarantee the quick-craft API can execute it.
- Skill-up colors and craft counts are estimated when the client has not
  observed the recipe at the required skill. Yellow crafts are not guaranteed.
- Missing market prices, reagent detail and unavailable rank/recipe records
  remain unknown rather than zero. AH prices are historical observations.
- Profession rank caps and required player levels still apply.
- No routes for gathering-based Mining/Herbalism/Skinning/Fishing are invented.
- The scope is 1-225 only; the skill cap may be 300. Do not invent 225-300 steps.

Please validate individual steps in the live Forever client before buying
large quantities of materials or publishing definitive costs.
