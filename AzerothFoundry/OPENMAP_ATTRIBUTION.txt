The MinimapTileIndex.lua coordinate-to-file-ID mapping is adapted from OpenMap/Data/Minimap.lua (generated from WoW client tables).
The coordinate grid convention is adapted from OpenMap/Map/Close.lua and OpenMap/Map/Stitch.lua.
OpenMap loads Blizzard-owned artwork from the game client. Azeroth Foundry
likewise uses in-client interface artwork where available; recovered client
BLP texture sheets are not distributed in this addon package.
